ARCHIVE PLANNER 2.0.0-alpha.1

Working product name. Read README.md before archiving valuable data.
Python 3.12+ is required. No third-party Python packages are needed to run.
Zstandard archives additionally need the zstd executable.

Windows: launchers\GUI.cmd or launchers\CLI.cmd
Linux:   launchers/GUI.sh or launchers/CLI.sh
macOS:   launchers/GUI.command or launchers/CLI.command (native qualification open)
CLI:     python3 archivist.py --help

Keep sources, evidence workspace, staging, replicas and restore output separate.
Choose --backend filesystem explicitly if you do not want Everything on Windows.
New restore output must not already exist.

This alpha is not physical-tape qualification. See docs/validation/README.md.
The complete source, specifications, tests and build scripts are included.
